## The problem

Who was struggling, with what, and why it mattered. Use a real number if you have one.

## My role

Who you worked with and which decisions were yours. Be specific about what you owned.

## Constraints

Time, tech, org, compliance. Constraints make the decisions interesting.

## What we tried

Two or three key decisions. For each: the options, what you chose, and why.
Show the work that changed your mind, not every artefact you produced.

![Describe what the image shows](./flow.png)

## Outcome

What shipped and what moved. Metrics, adoption, quotes. If it didn't ship, say what you learned.

> A pull quote from a user or stakeholder works well here.

## What I'd do differently

One honest paragraph. This is often what interviewers ask about first.
